credits them here and keeps their terms. Entries are normalized and filtered (whole-platform and whole-TLD rules,
and anything on the allowlist, are removed) before publication.

Discovery sources (GitHub, student link lists, urlscan.io, Certificate Transparency) supply individual hostnames
that are independently verified live before inclusion. The [Tranco list](https://tranco-list.eu/) is used only to
decide which discovered sites need human review, and none of its data is published.
